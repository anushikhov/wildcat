Analytical mechanics is a formulation of classical mechanics using scalar properties—primarily kinetic and potential energy—rather than Newtonian vector forces, to analyze complex systems. Developed from Lagrange and Hamilton's work, it utilizes variational principles (like the principle of least action) to derive equations of motion, offering powerful tools for constrained systems, rigid bodies, and celestial mechanics.   

Analytical mechanics provides a more advanced, elegant, and mathematically rigorous alternative to Newton’s laws, focusing on system constraints and coordinates.
