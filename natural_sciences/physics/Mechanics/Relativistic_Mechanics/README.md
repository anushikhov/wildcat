Relativistic mechanics describes the motion of objects traveling near the speed of light $(c),$ reconciling classical mechanics with Einstein's theories of Special and General Relativity. It replaces Newtonian mechanics at high velocities by incorporating frame-dependent time/space measurements, relativistic momentum, and energy-mass equivalence $(E = mc^2).$ Key principles include Lorentz transformations and the constancy of light speed for all inertial observers.
