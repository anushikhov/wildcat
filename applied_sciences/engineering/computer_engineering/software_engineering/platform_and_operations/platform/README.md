Platform Engineering builds and maintains internal developer platforms (IDPs) — reusable, self-service tools, abstractions, and workflows that empower application developers to ship faster with less friction (think "platform as a product"). Focuses on developer experience (DevEx), standardization, golden paths, and reducing cognitive load.   

Key technologies: Backstage, Crossplane, Terraform/Pulumi, Kubernetes operators, CI/CD templates, internal PaaS layers.  

Common domains: Large-scale organizations shifting from raw DevOps/Cloud to productized internal platforms; complements (but differs from) DevOps culture and SRE reliability focus.
